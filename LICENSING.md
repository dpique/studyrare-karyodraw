## Chromosome band data

`ideogram-data.js` is generated by `_build_inputs/build_ideogram.py` from the
UCSC hg38 `cytoBandIdeo` table, which UCSC makes freely available. It is
measured data rather than original expression, and nothing here adds a
restriction to it.
